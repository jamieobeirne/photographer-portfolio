## Lessons Learned

1. **Get hosting access from day one.** DNS changes made by the client without supervision caused hours of back-and-forth.
2. **Hostinger Account Sharing** is the clean solution — client goes to hpanel.hostinger.com → Account Sharing → Grant Access → enters your email. No password sharing needed.
3. **When DNS points to Vercel, WordPress can't be accessed via the main domain.** Always establish a direct WordPress admin URL that doesn't depend on the domain.
4. **DNS propagation + Vercel re-verification** are two separate steps. Sometimes a manual Refresh in the Vercel dashboard is needed after DNS propagates.
